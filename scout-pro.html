Gameplay

Scout.Pro is a fast paced arcade game about precision, you have to jump from platform to platform, evading all kind of obstacles along the way.

Art Style

Scout.Pro has a retro inspired art style, low resolution, crisp textures and minimalist UI, all inpired by 80's and early 90's aesthetics.

Platforms

Available for android devices.

Synopsis

You live in a space colony, your mission is to ensure the structural safety of this colony, your home.

To achieve such mission you are given a brand new scout robot, with it you will traverse through multiple areas of the colony, making sure that everything is working as expected.

Before doing that though, you'll need to complete the scout training program, 10 fast paced missions that will put you up to speed with this line of work. Move, jump, evade, dash, be ready for everything!
