What is Brunito Productions?

We are a couple that decided to make games.
We know this is no simple task, as games require a lot of organization, work, time and resources.
Still we are passionate and believe we have stories and experieces to share with the rest of the world. Our plan is to grow without stop, step by step and with the help of the community. We don't want to make games filled with ads, microtransactions or paywalls of any kind. We want to make games that are fun, that people can enjoy and they want to support.
We are regular people, tired of the endless slop that floods the industry these days, so even if our games are not the best selling of any platform, we still want to make them authentic.

Take a look at our first game - Scout.Pro

Sci-Fi Arcade Fun

Scout.Pro is a fast paced arcade game, reflexes are everything, expect a challenging experience.

Follow the beats of the music with your scout robot and complete colorful retro inspired levels.

The story will unwrap little by little, take notes of the messages you receive along the way.

The initial release has 10 levels packed with delightful frustration.
